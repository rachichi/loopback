2. Adoption of the June 2026 General Board Meeting Minutes.
The minutes of the June 2026 meetings was accepted on a vote of common consent with
no objections, to any necessary correction.  Corrected: April 2026 to May 2026.

3. Voting Session – SLA Applications
On a motion moved for a vote of common consent, the three below applications were
accepted with no objections from the Public Safety committee or the entire community
Board.

a)Lou’s Burger Flatbush, Inc d/b/s Lou’s Burger
b) Brooklyn Paradise LLC
c) Anjael Legacy Holdings, LLC d/b/a Anikii’s Café Lounge

4. New Business:
Board member Abdul Mutakabbir placed two motions on the floor; these were
seconded by Theresa Westerdahl.  #1.  Asking the board to review the City Charter on
Conflict of Interest into the matter of a fellow board member as their appointment to
the post of Vice Chair to a board Committee.  #2. Asking for the creation of a five-
member committee to investigate the leadership/chairperson of a board committee.  Both
motions were tabled.

5.  Adjournment. Chairman Ethan Norville called a motion for the adjournment of the
September General Board Meeting of Community Board 9 at approximately 9:30p.m.
All were in favor of the motion.
