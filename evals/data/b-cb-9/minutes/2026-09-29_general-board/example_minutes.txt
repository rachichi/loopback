4. District Manager’s Report:

• Public Notices
• The 2026 United Airlines Half Marathon is scheduled for March 15, 2026. Entry is
near Grand Army, so we should not see disruptions on Washington Avenue.
• FDNY: On January 29, 2026, at 35 Winthrop Ave, Brooklyn, a fire broke out at the
6story 40-unit multi-family building. No other tenants have been displaced at this time.
One fatality has been confirmed by FDNY. DOB issued a partial vacate for Apt 4D only
where the fire occurred and a DOB Summons for the fire.
 • MTA: On Monday February 2nd the OMNY Call Center has expanded its hours to 24
hours, 7 days a week. OMNY customer service representatives will be available 24/7 to
answer questions about tap and ride trips and transactions and to assist customers
with any inquiries or issues. Customers can call the OMNY Call Center at 877-789-6669
or chat with us online via the Digital Assistant.
 • DEP: NYC Department of Environmental Protection - The Community Right-to-Know
Law (Local Law 26 of 1988) protects public health and the environment by regulating
hazardous substances across New York City. Through the Right-to-Know (RTK)
Program, businesses such as automotive repair and dry cleaners must submit annual
Tier II reports detailing the type, quantity, and location of hazardous materials stored at
their facilities. Reports are filed through an online system with electronic payment
options, and the annual filing deadline is March 1.
• DOB: NYC Local Law No. 75, adopted in 2009, mandates updated visibility
requirements for roll-down security gates in most retail, office, and commercial spaces.
All affected gates must provide at least 70% visibility when fully closed. • Affected
Properties: Most commercial spaces, new and existing (excluding landmarks/historic
districts).
 • Compliance Deadline: July 1, 2026. All affected gates must be compliant by this date,
regardless of installation year. • Penalties: Non-compliance is a major violation.
• Gates installed after July 1, 2011, face fines starting at $250 for the first offense and at
least $1,000 for subsequent offenses. • Violations can be resolved if corrected and
certified with the NYC Department of Buildings (DOB) within 90 days.
 • DOT: A pilot program that aims to cut down on package thefts and reduce delivery
truck trips, LockerNYC is expanding into our district. The two locations where the
lockers will be placed are the following • 867 Nostrand Avenue at Mermaid Fish
Market**
• 614 Flatbush Avenue at Chester Pharmacy • 1294 Nostrand Avenue (new location)
 • DHCR: Clarkson Estates, state project, located at 329 Clarkson Avenue, Brooklyn, NY
11226 is opening the lottery for 163 lottery and 164 supportive units. Available units
range from 30% to 70% AMI. Deadline to apply is March 10, 2026 and the lottery will
be held at the end of March. You can request an application by emailing
clarkson@kgupright.com. Public Hearings

 • BKCB9: CB9 will be hold its annual Citywide Statement of Needs for FY27-28 on
February 18th, 2026, starting at 6:30PM at the Board Office. The primary purpose of
this document is to inform communities of the City’s facilities needs and the specific
criteria for selecting the locations of those facilities.

.NY: The Department of Sanitation (DSNY or the Department) is proposing to amend its
rules to include the implementation schedule for the Midtown South and Staten Island
Commercial Waste Zones (CWZ) and to create application process for infrequent
generators of commercial waste. DSNY will hold a public hearing on the proposed rule.
The public hearing will take place via Microsoft Teams at 10:00AM on March 5th, 2026,
using the following link: CLICK HERE TO JOIN HEARING. You can email written
comments to nycrules@dsny.nyc.gov. We are zone Brooklyn East with no
implementation date set.
